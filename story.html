The making process

From material
to furniture.

01

Measure

Dimensions come first. Every build begins by understanding what needs to be made and where it will live.

02

Prepare

Wood, metal, fabric, foam and other materials are prepared for the build.

03

Fabricate

Components are cut, shaped, welded, drilled or otherwise formed.

04

Assemble

Individual components become a complete piece.

05

Finish

Surface, upholstery, colour and finishing details bring the piece together.

06

Inspect

Before it leaves the workshop, the finished piece is checked.

The tools
matter.

The machines in the workshop, and what each one is for. Select one to look closer.

  1. Joins the frame

    Joining structural components and building the framework behind the finished piece.

  2. Prepares the parts

    Cutting metal and timber to size, so every component starts out accurate.

  3. Shapes the timber

    Shaping and drilling timber into tops, legs, rails and panels.

  4. Covers the piece

    Stitching covers and fitting fabric over foam and frames.

  5. The final surface

    Preparing and finishing surfaces — the last stage before a piece is ready. (Confirm exact methods.)
